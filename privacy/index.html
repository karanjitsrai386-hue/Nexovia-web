Privacy notice.

This site sets no cookies, runs no advertising trackers, and does not sell or share personal information. The only personal information it collects is what you type into the contact form. This page says exactly what happens to it.

Notice at collection · effective 19 August 2026

The short version.

What we collect, and why

If you fill in the contact form we collect your name, email, business name, phone number, sector, how many cameras you run, their makes and models, and whatever you write in the free-text field. We use it for one thing: to reply to you about Nexovia and, if you want one, to arrange a demo or a pilot.

Contact form · one purpose

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use it to build a profile of you, we do not run advertising pixels, and we set no cookies. There is no consent banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

No sale · no share · no cookies

Both are service providers, not buyers

Under the CCPA both act as service providers: they are contractually limited to processing the data for the purpose we engaged them for, and they are not permitted to retain, use, or disclose it for anything else. Disclosing personal information to a service provider for a business purpose is not a sale and is not a share.

Civ. Code § 1798.140

Legal and safety disclosure

We may disclose information if we are legally required to — a subpoena, a court order, or a binding request from a public authority — or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

Rare · on legal compulsion

GUARANTEED

How to make a request

Email prabhjeev@nexoviasecuritysolution.com with "Privacy request" in the subject line and tell us which right you are exercising. Because we operate entirely online and only hold what you gave us directly, email is the single method we need.

One email · no account required

What happens next

We confirm we received it within 10 business days and respond substantively within 45 calendar days. If the request is genuinely complex we may take one further 45 days, and we will tell you before we do, with the reason.

10 days to acknowledge · 45 to answer

Verifying it is you

We will ask you to send the request from the email address you originally contacted us with, and to confirm one or two details from your enquiry. We deliberately do not ask for ID documents — collecting a passport scan to prove a deletion request is worse for your privacy than the request itself.

No ID documents requested

Authorised agents

You may use an authorised agent. We will ask for written permission signed by you, and we may still contact you directly to confirm you authorised the request.

Written authorisation required
Scope

What this notice does not cover.

This page is about the website

It covers nexoviasecuritysolutions.com and the contact form on it. It does not cover video, detections, or clips captured by a Nexovia Hive installed at a customer's premises. That is a separate arrangement, governed by the contract between Nexovia and that customer.

Website only

If you were recorded by a Nexovia system

The business operating the cameras is the one that decides what is recorded, who can see it, and how long it is kept — and it is the right first point of contact. If you cannot reach them, email us at prabhjeev@nexoviasecuritysolution.com and we will help you identify the operator.

Contact the site operator first
