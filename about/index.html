The story

It started with a conversation about a camera that had already recorded everything.

Every small business owner has a version of the same story. Something happened — stock walked out, someone fell, a door was left open overnight — and the cameras caught all of it perfectly. Nobody saw it until the next day. The footage was excellent. The outcome was identical to having no cameras at all.

The industry's answer to that gap has been to sell a whole new system. New cameras, new cabling, a per-camera cloud subscription, a capital project. It is a good answer for a company with a facilities budget and a security team. It is not an answer at all for a store with twelve cameras, four staff and a payroll to meet on Friday.

So we built the other thing. One appliance that sits behind the cameras already on the wall, does the understanding on site, and speaks up while there is still time to act. Nothing replaced, nothing re-cabled, nothing shipped to somebody else's data centre to be looked at.

How we work

Four positions we are not flexible on.

01

Your footage does not leave your building to be understood

Inference happens on the Hive, on site. This is not a bandwidth optimization we happened to land on — it is a position about who should hold your video. It also happens to be why the latency budget is 23 milliseconds and why the system keeps working when your internet does not.

02

We do not make you throw away working equipment

Rip-and-replace is a great business model and a terrible deal for the customer. If a camera you bought six years ago still produces a usable stream, it should still be an asset. Nexovia is deliberately a layer, not a replacement.

03

We tell you what we cannot do

Some camera angles are too poor for any system to help with. Some detection classes are not ready. We would rather say so before you buy than have you find out in month three. Our capability page marks classes as shipping, pilot or roadmap for exactly this reason.

04

You get the knobs, not a black box

Every dwell time, zone and confidence threshold lives in your console, not in a config file only we can reach. Vendors who hide those settings end up with customers who mute the alerts instead of fixing them, and a muted system is worth precisely nothing.

Where we are

Honest about the stage we are at.

We are early. That is a real thing to weigh up when you are choosing a security vendor, so here is the state of it rather than a paragraph implying we have been doing this for a decade.

Built

The Hive runs, on real hardware, on a real site

Edge inference, the event engine, sealed clips, the encrypted tunnel, the web console and the mobile app are all working software today. Every clip on this site came off a live unit.

Shipping

Theft, falls, after-hours presence, tracking

These classes run today on a live Hive watching real cameras at our own site. The confidence figures shown across this site are the scores that unit actually produces — measured, not targets, and not a benchmark set. What we do not yet have is a customer store to point you at. That is what the pilot is for.

In pilot

Crowd density, tailgating, PPE, dock dwell

Working, and still being calibrated. Available to Business and Multi-Site customers who want to run them with us from the start.

Next

Weapon detection, spill, license plate, smoke

Built, not yet accurate enough on real-world angles for us to put in front of you as a promise. We will move them to shipping when the numbers justify it and not before.

What early access means for you

More attention than you would get later

Early customers get direct access to the people building this, tuning done by hand, and real influence over which detection classes we prioritize. That is the trade for coming in before the polish.

Talk to the people who will be on your site.

There is no sales layer between you and us. Whoever replies to your email is the same person who configures your Hive and picks up the phone when something looks wrong.
