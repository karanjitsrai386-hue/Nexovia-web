      The gap

      Recording is not security.
      It is paperwork.

      The average small business already runs eight to sixteen cameras. That footage is almost never watched live. It gets opened once — after the loss, after the fall, after the police ask for it — and by then the only thing it can do is confirm what you already lost.

      The market's answer has been to sell you an entirely new camera system. Rip out what works, re-cable the building, pay per-camera cloud fees forever. For a store with twelve cameras and a real payroll, that maths has never worked.

      Nexovia takes the other path. Every camera, cable and mount stays exactly where it is. We add the one part that was always missing — something that understands what it is looking at, and says so while there is still time to act.

      The signal path

      From lens to alert, end to end.

      Follow one frame all the way through. It leaves the camera over the Cat6 you already pulled, arrives at the Hive on a single uplink, gets decoded, inferred and rule-checked on-device, then surfaces as a live overlay and a resolved alert in the console.

      Swipe the diagram to follow the signal path

      01 — CAPTURE

      Your existing cameras

      Any IP camera that speaks RTSP or ONVIF. No firmware flashing, no swaps, no new mounts.

      02 — TRANSPORT

      The Ethernet run

      Streams travel the Cat5e/Cat6 already in your walls, through your PoE switch, into one uplink.

      03 — INFERENCE

      The Nexovia Hive

      Decode, GPU inference and the event engine all run on the box. Video never has to leave the building.

      04 — STREAM

      Live view with overlay

      Every feed, annotated in real time, on phone, desktop or a control-room wall.

      05 — ANALYTICS

      Console & alerts

      Sealed clips, plain-English search, and a push notification the second a rule trips.

      Keep every camera you already paid for.

      Nexovia is deliberately a layer, not a replacement. If your cameras produce an RTSP or ONVIF stream — and effectively all IP cameras do — the Hive will find them, pull them in and start reasoning about them. Your NVR keeps recording exactly as it does today.

      • Auto-discovery sweeps the subnet and lists every camera it can reach
      • Works alongside your existing NVR or VMS rather than replacing it
      • Per-camera model selection — theft on the aisle cams, falls in the back room
      • No firmware changes pushed to any camera, ever
      • Nothing to re-cable: the Hive rides the network you already own
