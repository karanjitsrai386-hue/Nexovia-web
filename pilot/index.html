A real site. A good pilot site, honestly described.

How a pilot runs

From your camera list to a working site.

Step 1 — Fit check

You send a camera list and one clip

Make, model, count, and footage from the angle you have least faith in. We run it through a Hive and come back with a written read: what we would flag, what we would miss, and which of your angles we would not put a promise on. If the answer is that we cannot help, that is where it ends.

Step 2 — Terms

We agree what the pilot covers

Scope, duration, which detection classes are in, what happens at the end, and the commercial terms. Written down before anything ships, so nobody is guessing later.

Step 3 — Install

The Hive ships, you plug it in

One free port on your switch and a power outlet, roughly ten minutes, no specialist needed. We do not send an engineer. From the moment it dials out we configure it remotely — discovery, zones, schedules, rules and escalation.

Step 4 — Live

Alerts start, thresholds settle

The first weeks are where a site finds its own false-positive personality. Every dwell time, zone and confidence cut-off is in your console to adjust, and we are on the other end when you want a second opinion on a setting.

Step 5 — Review

We look at the numbers together

What fired, what was real, what it caught that would otherwise have been found the next morning. That review decides whether the pilot becomes a normal deployment, and it is also the honest place to conclude it did not work for you.

What we ask of you

Four things, none of them onerous.

A pilot is not a free trial with the support switched off. It is a working relationship with a short list of obligations on our side and a short list on yours.

  • One named contact who will tell us when an alert was wrong
  • Permission to look at flagged clips with you when we are diagnosing a false positive
  • A short review at the end, honest about whether it earned its place
  • If it worked, a sentence we can quote — named or anonymous, your call

That last one is the only marketing ask in the programme, and it is genuinely optional.
