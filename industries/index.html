01 — Retail & convenience

Shrink is the entire business case.

A convenience store, an auto parts counter or a liquor shop already has cameras covering every aisle. What it does not have is anyone watching them during a shift. Nexovia turns those existing feeds into the thing the owner actually wanted when they bought them — something that says now, not something that confirms it afterwards.

  • Concealment and theft sequences on aisle cameras
  • Behind-the-counter and stockroom zones during trading hours
  • Register-area rules for voids and sweethearting patterns
  • After-hours presence on the storefront and rear door
  • Person-of-interest tracking for repeat offenders across visits

02 — Assisted living & care

The twenty minutes before someone walks past.

A fall found immediately and a fall found twenty minutes later are, medically, two different events. Cameras in communal areas and corridors already exist in most facilities for liability reasons. Pose-based fall detection turns that liability equipment into a response system.

The dwell window matters enormously here — a resident lowering themselves into a chair must never page a nurse. That threshold is the first one worth getting right on any care site, and it is yours to set.

  • Fall and prone-posture detection with configurable hold
  • No-motion dwell alerts in communal areas
  • Restricted room and plant-room entry
  • Exit-door monitoring for wander risk
  • Clip attached to every alert so responders arrive informed

03 — Warehouse, yard & logistics

Perimeter after hours. Process during them.

A yard has two completely separate security problems on the same cameras. Overnight it is a perimeter question, and any human inside the fence is the event. During the day it is an operations question — which bay has been occupied too long, who walked into the racking aisle without hi-vis, which door was propped open.

  • After-hours perimeter and fence-line presence, IR capable
  • Dock and bay dwell against expected turnaround windows
  • PPE compliance in designated zones
  • Restricted racking and plant-room entry
  • Propped-door and tailgating detection at controlled entries
